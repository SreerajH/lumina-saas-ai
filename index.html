FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is Lumina really free to start?
Yes — no credit card needed. The free plan includes 50 AI generations per month and 3 active projects. You can upgrade whenever you need more.
How is Lumina different from ChatGPT or Jasper?
Lumina combines writing, visual design, and automation in one tool, with a Brand Voice Lock feature that learns your specific tone. It's purpose-built for creators, not general use.
Can I use Lumina for client work?
Absolutely. Many agencies use Lumina to manage content for multiple clients. The Team plan lets you create separate brand profiles for each client with shared team access.
What integrations does Lumina support?
Lumina connects with WordPress, Webflow, LinkedIn, Instagram, Notion, Slack, and more. We're adding new integrations every month based on user requests.
Is my content and brand data private?
Yes. Your content and brand data is never used to train our models or shared with third parties. You own everything you create — always.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?
Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your billing period, then move to the free plan automatically.
